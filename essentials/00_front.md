# How to use the Essentials

This is the **first read** for ME 415. It is built for efficiency, not completeness: each chapter teaches only what you need to understand the topic and answer the questions that keep coming back.

Every chapter has the same shape:

1. **What to take from this chapter:** the recurring question types (archetypes), in order of marks, and the few formulas that carry the topic.
2. **One section per archetype:** the theory, enough to understand it, with the examined questions flagged in the text where the theory answers them (yellow **ASKED** boxes); then **one worked example** from a past paper and **one practice problem** from another past paper, with its answer.
3. **Theory questions asked:** a short list of every theory question set on the topic, pointing to the section that answers it.
4. **On one page:** the chapter's formulas, procedure and traps.

**What to have beside you.** A calculator, paper, and the **ME 415 Data Book** (`me415_databook.pdf`), the clean copy of the department data book and the tables attached to the papers. This document prints no tables; a lookup is written as "Data Book Table 15 (§D14): CLTD = 14 °C", where §D14 is the section of the ME 415 Data Book.

**Where to go next.** The **ME 415 Exam Guide** has one module per chapter here, with the full theory, every past term-final question solved, the source errata and two mock papers. A practice problem's full solution is cited as "Module N, Solution N.k".

## The paper

> **KEY: The paper, in five lines**
>
> - **Full marks 210, 3 hours.** Two sections of four questions; answer three from each.
> - **Since 2022-23 one question in each section is compulsory** (45 marks), plus two more from each section (30 marks each). The compulsory questions so far: **cooling load** (2022-23, 2023-24) and **fire protection** (2022-23, 2023-24).
> - **The topics carry nearly equal weight:** cooling load, lifts, fire, components and special refrigeration 12–13 % each; psychrometry and AC 11 %, vapour compression 11 %, absorption 7 %, ducts and refrigerants about 3.5 % each.
> - **About 59 % of the marks are theory** (short notes, comparisons, sketches) and 40 % numericals.
> - **About 79 % of all marks went to questions that had appeared before.** The cooling-load question (all 11 papers) and the multistage R134a question (10 of 11) are the same template every year with new numbers.

## Order of reading

Read chapters 1 and 3 first (the compulsory questions), then 2 and 4 (asked every year, one standard numerical each), then 5, 7, 9 and 8, and finally 6 and 10. Do not skip a topic: with compulsory questions and only two free choices per section, a missing topic can cost a whole question.
